## Prompts (as sent)

**A:** "Reference A — please generate an image: a product-design reference sheet for an original pair of vintage optician's glasses and their carrying case. Neutral light-grey studio background, soft even lighting, clean grid layout with generous spacing, orthographic-style views: (1) front view, (2) side view, (3) three-quarter view, (4) folded view, (5) the closed case, (6) the case open with the folded glasses inside. Glasses design: brushed gunmetal frame with subtle aged patina, softly squared smoke-grey lenses with a faint muted sea-green edge reflection, a double bridge, a small notch on the RIGHT hinge only, black temple tips. Case: hard clamshell, deep-navy textured exterior, worn ivory interior lining, small aged-brass clasp. Every view must show the identical design and proportions. Grounded stylized realism, clear practical geometry, tactile metal, glass and leather. No text, no labels, no logos, no watermark." This was preceded by one line introducing the project as an original VR survival-horror game.

**B:** "Reference B — please generate an image: a design reference sheet for an ORIGINAL FICTIONAL commemorative travel booklet insert from a fictional international cultural event called the Festival of Passports. It is a game prop, not a real passport, and must contain no official data. […] (1) closed booklet, deep-navy textured cover, abstract embossed compass-rose-and-lens emblem in aged gold foil, no words; (2) open flat: ivory insert page with fine engraved line patterns, a neat grid of ELEVEN empty oval seal spaces plus ONE separate rectangular return field at the bottom; (3) close-up of one seal space and the stitched gutter seam with thread fibres; (4) side view of page edges and stitched binding. Blank areas only: no letters, numbers, country names, flags, crests, barcodes, machine-readable lines or security data. Restrained dark-red ink only for thin abstract borders. […] No text, no logos, no watermark."

**C:** "Reference C — please generate an image: a character turnaround sheet for the game's single enemy, an original creature called the Passenger. Neutral mid-grey background, even studio lighting, three full-body views side by side at identical scale: front, side, back, standing in a neutral relaxed pose with arms slightly away from the body. Design: a tall, narrow humanoid about 2.2 metres tall, built from layered plates of mostly OPAQUE charcoal smoked glass, with thin reflective fracture edges catching pale sea-green light; the face is an empty smooth oval like a blank identification-photo frame, slightly recessed, with no features; a narrow horizontal slot cavity in the chest like a document slot; long thin fingers shaped like luggage tags; narrow, clearly separated limbs and joints that would be easy to rig. Restrained, elegant, unsettling, grounded stylized realism. NO horns, NO red eyes, NO gore, NO armour clutter, no glowing runes, no text, no watermark."

**D:** "Reference D — please generate an image: a character turnaround sheet for the protagonist, an ORIGINAL FICTIONAL Emirati man called Saeed, 26, a travel photographer. Short black hair, neat short beard, warm thoughtful expression, believable everyday proportions, wearing a plain off-white kandura with a simple collar and a worn camera strap across one shoulder. Neutral light-grey background, even studio lighting, three full-body views side by side at identical scale (front, side, back) plus one small head-and-shoulders close-up in a corner. Grounded stylized realism (not photoreal), consistent across all views. He must not resemble any real person or celebrity. No text, no logos, no watermark."
