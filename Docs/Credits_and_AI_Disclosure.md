## What is not in the game

- No real passport, ID number or machine-readable zone. The key art's passport-style cover shows stylised national lettering and an emblem; it is AI-generated artwork, not a reproduction of a real document (see `Docs/BRANDING.md`). The Festival of Passports, its booklet and every character are fictional.
- No partner or sponsor branding (no Abu Dhabi Gaming or Unity logos) and no claim of endorsement.
- No sacred symbols or religious items are used in the Passenger's disguises.
- No visual gore. The Passenger never touches the player. Its fingers stop short at the near miss.
- The soundscape is hard horror for adults since 18 Oct (screams, growls, bone cracks, drowning sounds): give it a content warning in the submission notes.

## Claims this project does not make

- No awards, sponsorship or partnership.
- No formal playtest: only Awaiz's own informal desktop play and the automated in-build tests listed in `Docs/QA/QA_Report.md`. Headset play has not been verified by a person (see `Docs/QA/FinalReleaseGate.md`).
